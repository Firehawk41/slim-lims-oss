ISO 17025 §7.6, Nordtest TR 537, from the lab's own QC. Precision uRw is the spread of a check standard's recoveries day to day; bias is the RMS of the CRM recoveries' and PT results' relative bias, with the reference values' own uncertainty u(Cref). U = k × √(uRw² + ubias²), k = {{ policy.k }}. Relative, as % of the value. Not on the report: the report's ± is still the two cups' half-difference.
