Nothing new on the other lenses:
- **1 Who may see it:** quality pages are staff with a quality capability (403 otherwise, tested); no customer data on them.
- **2 Who may do it:** managers decide and sign (checked in slim-quality and the app); personas hold only their CSV
  capability, never managing users (tested); self-approval allowed and shown (D-109).
- **7 Output escaping:** the SVG escapes every text (instrument names come from files); pages autoescape; no script,
  CSP unchanged (tested).
- **8 Module seams:** slim-quality reads slim-data only through the host (D-107); series are keyed by the
  instrument's and material's names as the files give them, so a rename starts a new series: accepted, documented.
  Every page recomputes the charts from the whole history (about 0.2 s on the 90-day demo); page or cache before
  years of real data.
- **9 Sessions and auth:** the quality router has CSRF (the sweep in `test_app.py` covers it).
- **10 Demo isolation:** each lab's seed migrates slim-quality into its own schema; `empty-public` drops the schema.
