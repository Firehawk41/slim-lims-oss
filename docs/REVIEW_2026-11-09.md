## Checked and sound (nothing new)

- **Who may see it (1):** customers see their own quotes and the default list (others 404, the PDF too), their own
  quote requests; screens show customer names only while the switch says no visitors; `/specs`, `/reference`,
  `/today/setup` are capability-gated; display links show no actions.
- **Who may do it (2):** pricing, lab displays, reference data, the quality capabilities and supervisors gate
  their pages; four eyes on spec changes (never the proposer); reset links and MFA resets respect "not above
  your own role" and never personas.
- **State says the truth (3):** a quote prices from its dates and withdrawal only; a spec approval is refused if
  the set changed since it was proposed; nothing here sets a TR status.
- **History is kept (4):** every new record table is append-only (quote requests and events, PDFs, sendings, TR
  quotes, reference changes, account events and messages, spec proposals and decisions); V5 withdrawals are
  rows added; released results keep the limits they were judged by.
- **Whose clock (5):** due dates, quote states and the reset link's expiry are the lab's day or aware instants.
- **Output escaping (7):** quote documents and PDFs, outbox messages, the day's board and the spec pages render
  through autoescaping templates; the QR code is server-made SVG; CSP unchanged.
- **Sessions and auth (9):** MFA login rotates the session; wrong codes share the password's lockout; a reset or
  password change signs older sessions out; every new router carries CSRF (the sweep covers them).

Accepted: a display link opens in the shared lab, or in the visitor's own lab within their browser (a demo
visitor's screen link doesn't work on another device); `lab_sections` and `display_links` are mutable
configuration (the Postgres audit log keeps their history); two approvals of one spec proposal at the same
moment: the second fails on the decision's unique key after an idempotent write.
